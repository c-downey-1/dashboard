Broiler Industry Executive Chartbook

Production

How much chicken the industry produces, whether growth comes from more birds or heavier birds, and where it is grown.

Chicken Production

Ready-to-cook broiler meat certified at federally inspected plants, total for the trailing 12 months

Chicken Production by Month

Ready-to-cook broiler meat by month: this year and last year as bars, the five-year average as diamonds

Production vs Same Month Last Year

Ready-to-cook production, % change from the same month a year earlier

Production, Year to Date

Ready-to-cook production, January to the latest month

More Birds or Heavier Birds

Trailing 12-month change vs a year earlier in birds slaughtered and in average live weight; together they give the change in live pounds

Average Live Weight

Average live weight of broilers at slaughter, monthly and 12-month average

Condemnations at Slaughter

Share lost at federal inspection, 12-month averages: birds condemned before slaughter (mostly dead on arrival) and carcass weight condemned after slaughter

Top Broiler States

Annual broiler production by state, live weight

Prices

Wholesale parts, retail feature prices and the live price received by growers' integrators.

Wholesale Chicken Prices

National weekly negotiated prices for domestic fresh product: major parts (FOB plant) and whole bird without giblets (WOG, delivered, all sizes)

Retail Chicken Feature Prices

National average advertised price, fresh, conventional, regular pack, 8-week average

Broiler Price Received

Monthly live-weight price received by producers, with the 12-month average

Hatchery Performance

Broiler hatchability: the long decline in the share of eggs set that hatch.

Broiler Hatchability

Weekly share of broiler-type eggs set that hatched, the average of reports in the trailing 52 weeks, and the 2012–2019 average of available reports

Hatchability by Year

Average of the weekly reports in each calendar year; the latest year to date, with last year over the same weeks

Hatchability Seasonal Comparison

Monthly average hatchability: this year, last year and the 2012–2019 average of available reports

Breeder Flock

Broiler-type hatching hens, their productivity, and the pullet pipeline behind them.

Broiler Breeder Flock

Broiler-type hatching layers on hand (first of month), with the change from a year earlier underneath

Breeder Productivity

Hatching eggs produced and chicks hatched per breeder hen per month, 12-month averages

Breeder Pullet Pipeline

Broiler-type hatching hens on hand vs the potential flock from U.S. pullet chicks placed 7–15 months earlier (USDA's measure; known up to 7 months ahead)

Chick Supply

Weekly eggs set and chicks placed: forward signals for meat supply. Chicks placed now reach slaughter in about 5–9 weeks, depending on bird size; eggs set hatch about 3 weeks later, so they lead by roughly 8–12 weeks.

Chicks Placed and Eggs Set vs Prior Year

Trailing 4-week total, % change vs the same weeks a year earlier

Weekly Chick Placements by Year

Broiler chicks placed for meat production, by USDA reporting week

Broiler-Type Chicks Hatched

Monthly broiler-type chicks hatched: this year, last year and the multi-year average

Feed and Margin

Feed is the largest cost of growing a broiler. These are price indicators built from corn and soybean meal futures, not measured costs or margins.

Corn and Soybean Meal Cost Component

Cost of the corn and soybean meal in one short ton (2,000 lb) of broiler feed, assuming 65% corn and 28% soybean meal; the other 7% of the ration is not included

Broiler-to-Feed Price Ratio

Pounds of the corn and soybean meal component that one pound of live broiler buys. Higher means live prices are strong relative to feed grain prices, all else equal

Storage and Trade

Frozen inventories and the export markets that set dark-meat value.

Frozen Chicken in Cold Storage

Total frozen chicken stocks (end of month)

Broiler Meat Exports

Monthly U.S. broiler meat exports

Avian Influenza

HPAI detections in flocks APHIS classifies as commercial broiler, broiler breeder or broiler breeder pullet. Same data as IAA's HPAI dashboard.

Broiler Birds Affected by HPAI

Birds in confirmed commercial broiler, broiler breeder and breeder pullet flocks, by month, with the number of flocks detected. Breeder flocks of unstated species are shown in grey
